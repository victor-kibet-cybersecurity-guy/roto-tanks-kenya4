About this catalogue

Roto Tanks Kenya Product and Enquiry Catalogue

A static catalogue built to help customers compare Roto product information, indicative prices and delivery enquiries before contacting by WhatsApp or phone.

What this website does

The website organizes water tanks, underground tanks, septic tanks, drums, sanitation, farming, road-safety and other moulded plastic products into searchable pages. Visitors compare available catalogue information, then request a current quotation by WhatsApp or phone.

Relationship to Roto Moulders Limited

This website does not claim to be the official corporate website of Roto Moulders Limited. The supplied background information states that Roto Moulders Limited was incorporated in Kenya in 1991 and that its factory and sales offices are off Enterprise Road in Nairobi's Industrial Area.

How prices are handled

Prices shown on the site come from the supplied catalogue dataset. They are presented as indicative references because current price, stock and delivery can change. Customers should request confirmation before ordering.

How quotations work

Product buttons prepare a WhatsApp enquiry containing the product name and listed price. Customers then provide their location and confirm current stock, dimensions, delivery, fittings and any other order details directly.

Who receives the enquiry

The WhatsApp and phone contacts shown on this catalogue receive the customer enquiry. The site has no login, cart, online checkout or payment processing.

Delivery arrangement

Delivery is handled through an enquiry. Customers should provide county, town and access information. The final quotation should clearly state the delivery cost and what is included before the customer proceeds.

Product information policy

Technical specifications, certifications, warranties, dimensions and installation requirements should only be treated as confirmed when supported by current manufacturer information. Where this catalogue does not have verified data, it tells the customer to confirm it instead of inventing a specification.
