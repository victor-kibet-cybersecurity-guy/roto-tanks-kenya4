How to Choose a Water Tank in Kenya

A buying framework for capacity, intended use, placement, water source, installation and quotation checks.

Define the job first

Decide whether the tank is for municipal backup, borehole storage, rainwater harvesting, livestock, construction, apartments or commercial use. The same capacity can perform differently depending on how quickly water enters and leaves the tank.

Choose above-ground or underground storage

Above-ground tanks are easier to inspect and generally simpler to install. Underground storage saves surface space but requires a product designed for burial and more site-specific installation planning.

Calculate capacity

Multiply daily demand by the number of reserve days, then add a reasonable buffer. Choose the next available size rather than a tank smaller than the estimate.

Inspect the installation site

Check foundation support, drainage, sunlight exposure, vehicle access, plumbing routes and maintenance space. Confirm manufacturer guidance for the exact model.

Compare quotation quality

A useful quotation identifies the exact product, capacity, price, stock, delivery charge and what is included. Avoid making a decision from a single headline price without checking the full delivered cost.

Compare the water source

Municipal supply, boreholes, rainwater and delivered water create different storage patterns. Municipal backup usually focuses on surviving interruptions. Borehole systems use a tank to buffer pump operation and peak demand. Rainwater harvesting needs enough storage to capture useful rainfall without oversizing beyond the roof catchment and household use. Delivered water often benefits from a tank large enough to receive economical delivery volumes.

Think about maintenance before installation

Leave space to inspect the tank, reach valves and work on connected pipework. Keep the lid or access point usable. Place overflow where it can discharge safely without eroding the base. If pumps are part of the system, position isolation valves so maintenance does not require draining every litre from storage.

Buying checklist

  • Intended use and water source defined.
  • Required capacity calculated.
  • Available footprint and delivery access measured.
  • Tank dimensions and installation instructions confirmed.
  • Price, stock and delivery confirmed in writing.
  • Warranty and technical documents requested where relevant.
