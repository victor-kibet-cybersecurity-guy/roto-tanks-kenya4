Delivery and access guide

Water Tank Delivery in Kenya

A clear process from selecting the tank to confirming road access, offloading, placement and plumbing responsibilities.

Delivery is planned after the exact product, quantity and destination are known. The tank price and transport price should be stated separately so you can see what is included.

Six-step delivery process

Choose product

Confirm capacity, vertical or underground type, height variant and colour.

Send exact location

Share county, town, map pin and a contact person who knows the route.

Confirm access

Measure gate width, height restrictions, bends, road surface and turning space.

Receive transport quote

Confirm vehicle, delivery charge, estimated timing and offloading responsibility.

Confirm order

Check stock, specifications, payment recipient, receipt and written warranty terms.

Arrange delivery

Clear the route, prepare the base and have agreed labour or lifting equipment ready.

Offloading and final placement

Delivery does not automatically include moving a tank from the truck to its final base. Small tanks may be handled by a suitable team, while large tanks can need additional labour or lifting equipment. Confirm who supplies ropes, handlers, crane or other equipment and who is responsible for damage during unquoted movement. The driver should not be expected to improvise a lift near overhead power lines.

Road access, gate width and overhead clearance

Measure the narrowest part of the complete route. Compare it with the exact dispatched tank diameter and height, then add handling clearance. Check estate barriers, arches, roof eaves, trees, cables, sharp bends, culverts, bridges, soft ground and the space required for the vehicle to turn or reverse. For construction sites, consider whether access will still exist on the scheduled date.

Who places the tank?

Agree a named handover point: roadside, inside the gate or on the prepared base. Placement can be separate from transport. A prepared base must be fully cured, level, smooth and large enough for the entire footprint. Do not put water into a tank sitting partly on blocks or unsupported edges.

Is plumbing included?

Assume plumbing is excluded unless the quotation lists it. Confirm inlet, outlet, overflow, drain, valves, connectors, pump, electrical work, rainwater filters and labour line by line. Outlet and lid dimensions can vary by product; use the exact model data sheet rather than cutting pipes from an estimate.

Before the vehicle is dispatched

  • Current stock and exact tank variant confirmed
  • Tank and delivery prices shown separately
  • Map pin, route and contact person supplied
  • Gate, height and turning measurements checked
  • Offloading team or equipment agreed
  • Base ready and final placement clear
  • Payment, receipt and warranty terms confirmed in writing

Delivery questions

Delivery enquiries can be made for locations across Kenya. Availability, route, vehicle and price must be confirmed for the exact destination and tank size.
