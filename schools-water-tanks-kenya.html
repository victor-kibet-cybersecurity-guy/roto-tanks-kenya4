Water Tanks for Schools and Institutions

Size institutional storage around pupil or user numbers, sanitation demand, meal preparation, cleaning and supply reliability.

Updated 18 August 2026Roto Tanks Kenya EditorialPeople-first buying guide

Daily demand has several components

Schools use water for drinking, handwashing, toilets, kitchens, cleaning and sometimes boarding facilities. Estimate each major use rather than applying a household figure to the whole institution.

Peak use is concentrated

Breaks, meal times and morning routines create short periods of high demand. Storage and plumbing should cope with these peaks.

Boarding schools need a larger reserve

Where users remain on site overnight, interruptions affect sanitation and meals more severely. Reserve planning should reflect the time required to restore supply.

Protect water quality

Secure covers, controlled access, clean collection systems and scheduled inspection matter in environments serving many people.

Plan for maintenance without shutting everything down

Large institutions can benefit from multiple tanks or isolation valves so one section can be serviced while another remains available.

Build a school water-use schedule

List the activities that consume water throughout a normal day. Toilets and handwashing create repeated peaks, kitchens may use water around meal preparation, and cleaning demand often rises after classes. Boarding facilities add bathing and overnight sanitation. This activity-based approach produces a more reliable estimate than using one number for every pupil.

Plan for emergency continuity

When a school loses water, sanitation and food preparation can be affected quickly. Decide what minimum services must continue during an interruption and size reserve storage around those priorities. A backup source, such as borehole or tanker delivery, changes the amount of storage required.

Protect access and hygiene

Keep tank lids, valves and pumps in controlled areas. Children should not climb tanks or access elevated structures. Schedule inspections of covers, overflow screens, pipework and bases as part of facility maintenance.
