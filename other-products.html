Specialized moulded products

This category brings together inspection chambers, hopper tanks, silo tanks, trailer tanks, square tanks and other specialized products. Each has different installation and handling requirements.

Inspection chambers

Inspection chambers support access to drainage or utility systems. Confirm dimensions, connections and installation compatibility with the planned system before ordering.

Hopper and silo tanks

These tank forms suit specialized storage or discharge arrangements. Capacity alone does not determine suitability. Ask for current technical specifications and outlet or support requirements.

Trailer and square tanks

Compact tanks offer portable or space-conscious storage options. Confirm how the tank will be restrained, supported and connected for the intended application.

Get the right specification

For specialized products, request the product code and manufacturer data before purchase. This reduces the risk of ordering an item that does not fit an existing system.

Product codes matter

Specialized moulded products often look similar while serving different functions. Use the product code when requesting a quotation so the supplier identifies the exact chamber, hopper, silo or trailer tank you need.

Confirm connection details

Inspection chambers and specialized tanks may need specific inlet, outlet or support arrangements. Ask for drawings or current technical data before fabricating connecting pipework or supports.

Transport and handling

Odd shapes sometimes require different transport and offloading arrangements from standard cylindrical tanks. Send quantity and destination together with the product code when asking for delivery.
