Choosing a water tank in Thika

Households, institutions, farms and businesses around Thika may use tanks for municipal backup, borehole storage or rainwater collection. Capacity planning should start with daily demand and reserve days.

Popular capacities to compare

Start with 1,000L to 3,000L for compact backup, 4,000L to 6,000L for many household uses, 8,000L to 10,000L for higher demand, and 16,000L to 24,000L for larger institutional, apartment, farm or commercial storage. The right capacity depends on your actual litres used per day.

Delivery enquiry information

Send your county, town or neighbourhood, preferred tank capacity and any access constraints. Current delivery availability, cost and timing should be confirmed before ordering.

Prepare the site first

Confirm tank dimensions before building the base. Make sure the support is stable and level, and check the delivery route from the road to the final position.
