How Long Does 5,000 Litres of Water Last?

A 5,000L tank lasts 10 days at 500L/day, 5 days at 1,000L/day and 20 days at 250L/day. Use your real daily consumption for planning.

Simple formula

Days of storage = tank capacity ÷ daily water use.
