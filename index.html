Before you order

Know what happens before payment and delivery

Quotation

Send the product, capacity, quantity and delivery location. Current price and stock must be confirmed before you proceed.

Delivery charge

Delivery depends on tank size, quantity, destination, access and current logistics. Ask for the amount in writing.

Specification check

Confirm capacity, dimensions, fittings and installation requirements against current manufacturer information.

Payment

Use only the payment method provided in the confirmed quotation. Verify recipient details before sending money.

Receipt or invoice

Ask how your receipt or invoice will be issued before payment if documentation is required.

Stock unavailable

If the requested size is unavailable, ask about the nearest suitable capacity rather than accepting an unverified substitute.

Warranty

Ask for the current manufacturer or seller warranty terms and the claim process before purchase.

Returns and cancellations

Read the returns and cancellation information before payment, especially for delivered or special-order items.
