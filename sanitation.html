Waste volume determines bin size

The catalogue ranges from compact sanitary and dust bins to 690L and 1,100L mega bins. Estimate the amount of waste produced between collections and choose capacity with enough buffer for peak periods.

Mobility and pedal operation

Wheel bins help move waste between collection points. Pedal models reduce hand contact with the lid, which can suit controlled hygiene environments. Confirm the exact product configuration before ordering.

Waste separation

The listed 3-in-1 bin supports separated waste streams. Clear labelling and consistent placement matter as much as the container itself when a site wants users to sort waste correctly.

Handwashing and sanitation products

The catalogue also contains handwash products and other moulded sanitation items. Confirm capacity, fittings, intended use and cleaning requirements for the specific product.

Commercial buying

Schools, offices, institutions and property managers should consider bin placement, collection frequency, cleaning access and spare capacity before choosing sizes.
