Who needs 10,000 litres

This size targets sites where several users share water or where supply interruptions need a larger buffer. Apartments, schools, farms and businesses often reach this capacity range sooner than single households.

Demand calculation matters

A 10,000L tank lasts ten days at 1,000L daily demand, but only five days at 2,000L per day. Estimate peak-use periods as well as average demand.

Space, foundation and delivery

Large tanks require a suitable base and clear access. Confirm external dimensions, gate clearance, turning space and offloading before dispatch.

10,000L demand examples

A building using 1,500 litres each day has roughly six and a half theoretical days of storage in a 10,000L tank. At 2,500 litres daily, reserve falls to four days. For apartments and schools, use peak occupancy and busy-day demand rather than a quiet-day estimate.

One 10,000L tank or two smaller tanks?

Two smaller tanks can offer redundancy and easier placement, while one large tank simplifies some plumbing arrangements and uses fewer connections. The better configuration depends on the site, available footprints, pump arrangement and budget. Multiple tanks also allow one tank to be isolated for maintenance while the other remains in service.

Large-tank delivery preparation

Check gate clearance, overhead obstructions, turning space and where the delivery vehicle can stop. Prepare the base before dispatch. Confirm who handles offloading and whether equipment or extra labour is required. These logistics should be resolved before the tank leaves the supply point.
