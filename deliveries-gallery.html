Verified Tank Deliveries

A transparent gallery structure for real completed orders—without fabricated reviews, photos or customer details.

Verification first

No delivery record is published without evidence and permission

This gallery is ready for genuine completed-order photos, but no customer review, location, purchase month or delivery image has been invented to fill the page. Entries will be added only when the business can verify the order and has permission to publish the image and limited location information.

What each verified delivery entry will show

Product

Exact tank type and capacity, such as a 10,000L vertical Roto tank.

Delivery context

Town or broad customer area, property type and access note without exposing a private address.

Date and evidence

Purchase month, completed-order or installation image, and customer publishing permission.

Photo and testimonial checklist

  • Use only original photos owned by the business or supplied with clear permission.
  • Remove house numbers, faces, number plates and precise location details unless explicitly approved.
  • Record tank capacity, delivery town, property type and month from the actual order.
  • Quote customers accurately and keep consent with the order record.
  • Use Google review screenshots only when authentic and allowed by the platform and reviewer.

Example entry format—not a customer claim

Template: [10,000L Roto tank] · Delivered to [town] · [Residential installation] · [Month and year] · [Verified photo]

The bracketed example above demonstrates the required format only. It is deliberately not presented as a completed delivery.
