Kiambu County buying guide

Water Tanks in Kiambu

Compare water storage for suburban homes, rental developments, schools, farms and businesses across Kiambu’s fast-growing urban and agricultural areas.

Kiambu combines dense apartment corridors with standalone homes, coffee and horticultural farms, schools and roadside businesses. A rental block near Ruiru has a different demand pattern from a Limuru homestead or Gatundu farm. Count occupied units and peak demand instead of choosing from plot size alone.

Popular tank capacities in Kiambu

2,000L to 5,000L sizes suit many homes; 6,000L to 10,000L is often compared for rentals and schools; farms may need multiple tanks or 16,000L-plus storage tied to actual irrigation demand.

Use daily demand × desired backup days as the starting calculation, then allow for future occupants, water-source reliability and usable installation space. For example, five people using 50–100 litres each per day consume roughly 250–500 litres. A 5,000L tank may therefore provide about 10–20 days if no water is added, but irrigation, laundry, guests and leaks reduce that period.

Delivery access in Kiambu

New estates may have unfinished roads, sharp entries and soft shoulders, while older compounds can have narrow masonry gates. Tanks for upper-floor or rooftop positions require a separate lifting and structural plan. Share landmark directions because duplicated estate names can send delivery vehicles to the wrong approach road.

  1. Send the exact map pin and a reliable phone contact.
  2. Measure the narrowest gate, arch, driveway bend and overhead restriction.
  3. Confirm the tank’s dispatched diameter and height before the vehicle leaves.
  4. Agree who will offload, move and place the tank on the prepared base.
  5. Keep the route and final placement area clear on the delivery day.

Rainwater and backup storage

Higher-rainfall parts around Limuru and the Aberdare-facing slopes can make roof harvesting productive, but storage should still be sized from catchment and use. In drier eastern areas, a larger reserve may be more valuable when borehole or piped supply is intermittent.

A tank should have a stable, smooth, continuous base, a screened inlet where appropriate, controlled overflow and independently supported pipework. A full tank weighs about one kilogram for every litre stored, before the tank and fittings are added. Elevated structures need professional design for the full water load, wind and maintenance access.

Products commonly requested

Above-ground vertical tanks handle general domestic and business storage. Rainwater projects may also need leaf screens, first-flush diversion and overflow pipework. Sites with very limited surface space can compare purpose-built underground tanks, while wastewater systems require septic products and site-specific installation guidance. Do not bury an above-ground vertical tank.
