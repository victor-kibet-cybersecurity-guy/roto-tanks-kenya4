Best Water Tank for Borehole Storage

Borehole storage planning should consider pumping schedule, household or farm demand, water quality, tank siting and reserve capacity.

Start with demand

Estimate daily use, multiply by the number of backup days you want, then choose the next practical listed size above the result.

Check the site

Measure gate access, placement area and base space. Large tanks need enough clearance for delivery and positioning.

Confirm before ordering

Ask for current dimensions, technical specifications, price, stock, delivery charge and installation guidance.
