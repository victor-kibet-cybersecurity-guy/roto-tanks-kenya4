Best Water Tank for Borehole Water

Choose borehole storage by capacity, water chemistry, pumping schedule, site placement and verified material suitability.

Match storage to the pump and demand

Borehole storage works as a buffer between pumping and consumption. Compare pump yield, pumping hours and daily demand so storage does not empty faster than the source can replenish it.

Test water chemistry

Borehole water quality varies. Use water testing and verified manufacturer material guidance when storing water with unusual mineral or chemical characteristics.

Protect the tank and plumbing

Use secure covers, appropriate inlet and overflow arrangements and a stable support. Avoid plumbing loads that pull on the tank outlet.

Choose capacity from interruption risk

If pumping depends on electricity, storage also protects against power interruptions. Decide how many hours or days of water you need when pumping stops.

Above-ground versus underground

Above-ground storage simplifies inspection. Underground storage saves surface space but requires a tank specifically designed for burial and careful site installation.

Match tank volume to borehole yield

Suppose a borehole produces water slowly but steadily. A storage tank allows the pump to run during low-demand periods and build a reserve for morning or evening peaks. If daily demand exceeds what the borehole produces over 24 hours, a larger tank does not fix the underlying shortage. Storage redistributes supply over time but does not create water.

Control pumping intelligently

Level switches or other controls can protect against overflowing and help automate pump operation. The exact control system depends on the pump, borehole and tank arrangement. Include dry-run protection and competent electrical installation where appropriate.

Water testing matters

Borehole water may contain minerals, salts or other constituents that affect taste, staining, plumbing or treatment needs. Test the water through an appropriate laboratory or professional service, then confirm the storage material and treatment system suit the results.
